Configure approval emails

Uptempo emails approvers about approval requests that are waiting for their review. As an admin, you choose how approvers get these emails: an email as soon as each request is submitted, a daily or weekly digest, or both.

Before you begin

  • Activity approvals must be enabled in your Uptempo instance. This feature is in Preview; contact your Customer Success Manager to enable it.

  • To change approval email settings, you must be an admin who can configure approval processes.

How approval emails work

Email options

Approval email settings apply to your whole organization and to every approval process. You can't set different options for a single process or user.

Immediate emails

Each assigned approver gets an email with a link to the request on the Approvals page as soon as it's submitted.

Daily digest

Each approver gets one email a day listing the new requests submitted for their review.

Weekly digest

Each approver gets one email a week summarizing their open requests.

You can turn on immediate emails, a digest, or both.

Emails are notifications only. Approvers approve and deny requests on the Approvals page or from the activity's details panel on the Timeline, not from an email. Uptempo doesn't email submitters when a request is approved or denied; the decision and any comment are shown on the approval request.

Daily digest

The daily digest covers every request submitted for an approver's review since the last digest, across all approval processes.

  • New submissions and resubmissions after a denial are included.

  • Requests that have already been decided before the digest is sent, by any approver, are left out.

  • Each request shows the activity name, submitter, and due date.

  • A button opens the Awaiting my review tab on the Approvals page.

  • No email is sent when nothing new was submitted for that approver.

Weekly digest

The weekly digest summarizes each approver's open work.

  • Open requests are counted as Overdue, Approaching deadline, or On track, using the same deadline rule as the Approvals page.

  • The 10 requests with the soonest due dates are listed, followed by a link to the Approvals page.

  • No email is sent to anyone who has no open requests assigned to them currently.

Send times and time zones

You set one send time for the daily digest and one for the weekly digest. Each time uses the time zone of the admin who saves the setting, and the digest goes out at that moment for everyone in your organization, regardless of where they are.

Example

An admin in Denver sets the daily digest to send at 7:00 a.m. Approvers in Denver get the digest at 7:00 a.m. local time, approvers in New York get it at 9:00 a.m. local time, and approvers in London get it at 2:00 p.m. local time.

Change approval email settings

Choose approval email options and digest times

  1. In the navigation menu, click Approvals.

  2. On the Approvals page, click Open approval settings.

  3. In the settings menu, under General Settings, click Email Digest.

  4. Turn Immediate emails on or off.

  5. Optional: Turn on a digest, then set its send time:

    • Daily digest: Set the time of day the digest is sent.

    • Weekly digest: Set the time the digest is sent each week.

  6. Click Save.

Your settings apply to the whole organization with immediate effect. The next digest goes out at the time you set.
